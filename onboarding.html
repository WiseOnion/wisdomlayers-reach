Onboarding Guide

Welcome to WisdomLayers

WisdomLayers is a web design agency building custom websites for small businesses across North Carolina. Your job: find businesses that need a website, start conversations with them, and hand them off when they're ready to talk. We close the deal, you earn a commission.

Who We Target

Location: Small businesses in North Carolina only.

Type: Local service businesses, barbershops and hair salons, contractors (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, pressure washing), cleaning companies, restaurants and food businesses, therapists and counselors, auto repair shops, landscapers, photographers and videographers, or any local service business a homeowner or resident would search for.

Criteria: They must have either no website at all, or a website that is clearly outdated (looks like it was built before 2015, not mobile-friendly, broken pages, no contact form).

Skip: Any business with a modern, functional website. Emailing them makes us look uninformed.

How to Find Businesses

Start with your own research before using the provided lead list.

Where to look:

  • Google Maps: search "[service type] in [NC city]" and look at businesses with no website linked or a bad one
  • Yelp: same approach, filter by city and service category
  • Facebook: search for local NC businesses with only a Facebook page and no website
  • Chamber of Commerce directories for NC cities

How to verify a business needs a website:

  1. Search their business name on Google
  2. Click any website link that comes up
  3. If there is no website, they are a target
  4. If the site looks like it was built in 2010, loads slowly, or isn't mobile-friendly on your phone, they're still a target

Track every business you research in the Lead Tracker, even if you decide not to contact them. Label their website status so we never research the same business twice.

Once you've found one, research it before writing. Your goal is one or more genuine, verified observations that make the email feel written specifically for them, not a guess. Full checklist and examples on the Email Scripts page's Research tab. If you can't find anything meaningful, skip the lead.

Skip the lead if: you can't verify key information, can't find a meaningful way to personalize the email, the business recently asked not to be contacted, it's permanently closed, or it already has a modern, well-maintained website with nothing worth mentioning. Quality beats quantity.

How to Write Outreach Emails

Full scripts on the Email Scripts page

Do not use copy-paste templates. Every email must reference something specific about that business. Full outlines with examples for each lead type live on the Email Scripts page, this is the short version.

The structure (Problem-Impact-Solution-Question):

✎ Outline, write your own version from this
Problem
One specific, real observation about their business, something you actually saw, not a guess.
Impact
Explain one likely business impact, without exaggerating. Only state it if you'd stand behind it, otherwise soften with "probably" or "likely," or skip this part.
Solution
One sentence introducing WisdomLayers. No price.
Question
One soft question, no pressure into a meeting.

Rules:

  • Keep every email under 150 words, business owners skim
  • Never mention price in the first email, that's a discovery call topic
  • Never say "I hope this email finds you well"
  • Never use the word "synergy" or any corporate buzzword
  • End with one question only, never two
  • Always reference something real and specific about THAT business
  • Verify every fact before mentioning it, never guess or invent details

When Someone Replies

If they reply politely but aren't ready yet: "Thanks." "We'll keep you in mind." "Maybe later." That's a Positive Response (tracked as "Replied" in the tool), interested enough not to ignore you, not ready for the owner. Update the tracker to Replied and log it, no handoff needed yet.

If they're interested or asking questions: Immediately update their status in the tracker to "Warm Lead" (see the Warm Leads tab). Reply within one hour whenever possible, fast responses consistently outperform delayed ones. Write your own reply, short and conversational, actually answer whatever they asked, don't pitch hard or discuss price. Notify the owner right away and tell the lead the owner will follow up.

If they say not interested: Reply "No worries at all, appreciate you taking the time to respond." Update the tracker to Not Interested. Move on.

If they say unsubscribe: Reply "Of course, removing you now." Update the tracker to Unsubscribed. Never contact them again from any WisdomLayers email address. This is a legal requirement.

What a Warm Lead Means

A warm lead is a business owner who replies with genuine interest. They ask a question, want more information, or are open to a conversation. Email opens and link clicks are useful signals, but they are not warm leads. Only a real reply counts.

You hand off a warm lead by:

  1. Updating their status in the tracker to "Warm Lead", this automatically alerts the owner
  2. Messaging the owner directly with the business name, a summary of what they said, and their preferred contact method
  3. Replying within one hour whenever possible, fast responses matter more than polished ones

Do not try to close the deal yourself. Your job ends at the handoff.
