Outcome Demo

Not wired to any workspace or backend - this is the same outcome picker from the Dashboard, running on one fake lead so you can click through all five outcomes and see exactly what each one shows.

What each outcome actually does

They replied
Opens a note field for what they said, plus an optional screenshot. Saving commits status → Replied. Stays in My Leads - there's usually a follow-up conversation still to have.
Warm lead
Shows a generic handoff script - addressed to your team lead/owner, not the prospect. Fill in the brackets (business name, contact info) yourself, then copy and send it up. Clicking Done commits status → Warm Lead and surfaces the amber "notify your team lead now" banner. Stays in My Leads until the handoff closes out.
Not interested
Opens a private note field (why - not sent anywhere) plus a courtesy script to copy. Saving commits status → Not Interested. This is a true dead end - moves to History.
No reply
Shows a copyable follow-up check-in script for a second attempt. Clicking Done commits status → No Reply. Not a dead end, just means try again later - stays active in My Leads as a follow-up nudge.
Unsubscribed
No form, no script - unambiguous the moment it's clicked, so it commits immediately, status → Unsubscribed. True dead end - moves straight to History.
